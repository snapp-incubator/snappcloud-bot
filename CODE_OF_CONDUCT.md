# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward, respectful
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or
employer.

## What we expect

- Engage with the technical substance. Disagreement is welcome; dismissiveness
  is not.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding they
  were careless.
- Accept review as being about the change, not about you — and give review the
  same way.
- Be patient with people learning the system. Most of it is not obvious, and
  much of it is documented only in commit messages.

## What we will not accept

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing others' private
information, and any conduct that a reasonable person would find unwelcome in a
professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers, privately, through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/snapp-incubator/snappcloud-bot/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the SnappCloud team directly. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainers will respond as promptly as they can.

Maintainers who do not uphold this in good faith may be removed from the
project.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project elsewhere.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
